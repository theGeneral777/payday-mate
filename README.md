# Payday Mate

Australia's peer-to-peer social trust lending network — built for everyday Australians who want to help each other out between paydays.

---

## What is Payday Mate?

Payday Mate is a P2P lending network where members loan money to each other based on their payday cycles. When you get paid, you can lend to a member who needs it now. When their payday arrives, they pay you back directly. Simple, social, and built on trust.

No banks. No middlemen. No money moves through the platform. Members settle peer-to-peer using **PayID** — instant, direct, and free.

Payday Mate is not a regulated financial product. It is a social trust network that connects everyday Australians — full-time workers, shift workers, gig workers, and everyone in between.

---

## How It Works

### 1. Join and set your payday cycle
Sign up and enter when you get paid — weekly, fortnightly, or monthly. This is the heartbeat of the network.

### 2. Browse or request a loan
When you need a hand, post a loan request. When you're flush, browse open requests and lend to a member you trust.

### 3. Settle directly with PayID
All money moves between members via PayID — bank-to-bank, instant, and completely outside the platform. Payday Mate never touches your funds.

### 4. Build your trust score
Every on-time repayment lifts your trust score. Every late or missed repayment lowers it. Your score is your reputation on the network — the higher it is, the more lenders want to work with you.

### 5. Repay on your payday
Repayment is due on the borrower's next payday. The cycle is clear, predictable, and built into the network from day one.

## Subscription Tiers

Payday Mate runs on a weekly subscription. One flat fee covers unlimited transactions at your tier — no per-loan charges, no hidden fees.

| Tier | Price | Active Loans | Key Perks |
|------|-------|-------------|----------|
| **Silver** | $2.99 / week | Up to 2 | Access to the network, basic matching |
| **Bronze** | $5.99 / week | Up to 5 | Trust badge, repayment reminders |
| **Gold** | $9.99 / week | Unlimited | Priority matching, dispute mediation |
| **VIP** | $14.99 / week | Unlimited | All Gold perks + featured profile, early alerts, dedicated support |

All prices are in AUD. Subscriptions are processed securely via Stripe.

---

## Dispute Mediation

Gold and VIP members have access to Payday Mate's dispute mediation service. If a repayment goes wrong, our team facilitates communication between both parties to reach a resolution. We document outcomes — we do not adjudicate financial claims or move money on anyone's behalf. All settlements remain between members via PayID.

---

## Trust Scores

Every member has a trust score, visible to the network. Scores are built through:

- On-time repayments
- Consistent lending history
- Member feedback

Trust scores are the platform's core differentiator. They reward reliable members and protect lenders from repeated bad actors.
