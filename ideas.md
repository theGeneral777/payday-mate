# Payday Mate design direction

## Three initial directions

### Theme Name: Whiteboard Trust
Very Brief Intro: A crisp white-first community landing page using multicolour signals, generous whitespace, and editorial structure to make peer-to-peer lending feel open and understandable.
Probability: 0.07

### Theme Name: Payday Noticeboard
Very Brief Intro: A tactile, paper-inspired direction with pinned-note details, warm utility colours, and a friendly neighbourhood tone for everyday Australians.
Probability: 0.04

### Theme Name: Signal & Settlement
Very Brief Intro: A more energetic product direction built from bold colour blocks, clear transaction signals, and lightweight motion that makes the payday cycle feel active and dependable.
Probability: 0.02

## Chosen approach: Whiteboard Trust

### Design Movement
Contemporary Swiss-inspired editorial design softened by community noticeboard warmth: precise hierarchy, open margins, and colour used as a functional signal rather than decoration.

### Core Principles
1. White space is the primary trust cue; surfaces remain bright, calm, and easy to scan.
2. Blue, red, yellow, and green are used as distinct semantic accents across trust, action, timing, and community moments.
3. Content is arranged in an asymmetric editorial flow rather than a generic centered marketing grid.
4. Every interaction should make the next safe action obvious without implying that Payday Mate holds or moves money.

### Color Philosophy
The base is white with deep ink-teal typography for clarity and reassurance. Google-inspired blue, red, yellow, and green accents are deliberately separated so the brand feels optimistic and recognisable without resembling a financial-institution dashboard. Teal is reserved for structural action and legal trust cues; colour accents mark energy and community.

### Layout Paradigm
A left-led hero with a floating brand card establishes the story first, followed by an evidence strip, an editorial five-step flow, tier cards, and a community callout. The layout should feel like a well-designed noticeboard that can be read top-to-bottom or navigated by anchor links.

### Signature Elements
1. The multicolour handshake and Payday Mate wordmark on white.
2. Small colour-dot eyebrow labels that act as section wayfinding.
3. Rounded membership cards with colour-coded tier rails rather than heavy gradients.

### Interaction Philosophy
Interactions should be clear and low-pressure. Buttons use direct language, gain a small lift on hover, and preserve keyboard-visible focus. Where a live Whop or Discord URL is not available, the interface explains the pending state rather than inventing a destination.

### Animation
Use short, ease-out transitions for buttons and cards. Reveal content with subtle opacity and translate motion only when the user has not enabled reduced motion. Avoid looping motion and avoid animating financial values or trust scores in ways that could feel promotional or misleading.

### Typography System
Use a rounded display face for major headlines and a neutral readable sans-serif for body copy. Headlines use tight tracking and mixed colour emphasis; body copy stays at a comfortable reading width. Navigation and labels are compact, uppercase, and letter-spaced to support scanning.

### Brand Essence
Payday Mate is a peer-to-peer Australian community for people who lend when they are flush and borrow when they need a hand, built around payday cycles, PayID settlement, and visible trust. Personality: **open, practical, neighbourly**.

### Brand Voice
Headlines are plainspoken and warm. CTAs are direct and useful rather than salesy. Microcopy is candid about the platform’s boundaries and keeps repayment and communication visible.

Example lines:

> Lend when you're loaded. Borrow when you need it.

> No bank. No middleman. Just community trust and your PayID.

### Wordmark & Logo
The logo keeps the original handshake silhouette, with each hand and lettering using the four-colour accent system. The wordmark is rounded and friendly, while the slogan sits beneath in dark ink for legibility on white.

### Signature Brand Color
**Payday Teal — #07585C.** It anchors actions and structural details without overpowering the multicolour mark.

## Style Decisions

- Keep all major branded surfaces white or near-white.
- Do not use a purple gradient or dark neon treatment.
- Keep external integrations visibly honest: live URLs are placeholders until supplied.
- Use the exact slogan: “Lend when you're loaded...borrow when you need it!”
