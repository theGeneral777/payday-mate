## Monetization options

### Option A — External membership checkout (fastest)

Use the existing four membership tiers through the configured external checkout provider, then place the live checkout link in the channel description, pinned post, and website:

- Silver — $2.99/week
- Bronze — $5.99/week
- Gold — $9.99/week
- VIP — $14.99/week

This requires the operator to supply the real checkout URL(s). Do not publish placeholder links.

### Option B — Telegram-native digital subscriptions

Telegram supports Stars-based subscriptions for channels and bots. This requires creating/configuring a bot or paid channel through the owner account, selecting the Star price, and complying with Telegram's applicable creator and payment terms. Star pricing is not automatically equivalent to the Australian-dollar membership prices above.

### Option C — Telegram channel ad revenue

Eligible public channels may receive a share of advertising revenue. This is platform-controlled and should be treated as supplemental revenue, not guaranteed income. It is not an immediate replacement for membership checkout.

1. Create the public channel and choose its final username.

4. Supply the live checkout URL or configure Telegram Stars privately in Telegram.
